<h1>California Jones</h1>
Description:
The game starts with a player on a field surrounded by walls and the goal of the player is to reach its destination in the least amount of time avoiding the obstacles. Hitting an obstacle can lead to a penalty of 10 seconds. Players would be alerted by different sounds while encountering obstacles and walls. The player would also be alerted if it is near the destination.

Tools Used:
Unity Platform
